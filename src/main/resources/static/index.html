Method

Numbers first. Conclusions second.

GambitRate is an opinionated read of the games you have already played. It looks for a pattern in your results, then checks whether there are enough games to trust it.

How we decide what to flag

We compare each opening with your overall win rate for the same color. An opening is only flagged when it has at least five games and the difference is very unlikely to be luck. Openings with fewer games are hidden.

What "rating impact" means

We compare your win rate in an opening with your usual win rate, then scale the difference by how many games you played. The result is an estimate in rating points: a negative number means you are losing rating there, and a positive one means you are gaining. It is a guide for ranking problems, not an official rating change.

What we don't claim

We do not use a chess engine, so we can't say which single move lost a game. A low clock does not prove a mistake. We only show accuracy when chess.com supplied it.
